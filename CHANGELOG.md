# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-08

Initial release of the from-scratch Melbourne miniature.

- Geography: the Yarra channel with two inhabited banks, the Port Phillip Bay wedge, and a
  24.4 × 18.4 rounded tray.
- Plan: the Hoddle Grid and named streets, St Kilda Road, 2,100 road edges, three bridges, 291
  building parcels and one connected graph.
- Eight landmarks: Flinders Street Station, Federation Square, Eureka Tower, the Arts Centre
  Spire, the Royal Exhibition Building, the MCG, St Paul's Cathedral and the Shrine of
  Remembrance.
- Nine ordinary building families, with a low-rise riverfront and distinct district palettes.
- Transport: seven trams with route groups, 35 other vehicles, four river/bay boats and 44
  pedestrians.
- Day/night lighting with warm windows, gold landmark emission, lamp pools and river reflections.
- English and Vietnamese copy, a compact layout and a 345 × 345 embed.
- Verification: 2/2 test suites and 20/20 deterministic browser checks.
- Release: one 896 KiB self-contained HTML file; 1.1M triangles and 175 draw calls at the default
  overview. `docs/index.html` is the Pages-ready copy.
- Published to GitHub Pages: <https://somphol.github.io/melbourne-miniature/>.
